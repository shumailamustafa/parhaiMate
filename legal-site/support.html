Support & Data Deletion

Contact

For help, bug reports or feedback, email moazzamsamoo819@gmail.com. Please include your Android version and the app version (Settings → App Version).

Delete your data

ParhaiMate has no accounts and keeps no data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. All of your data is on your phone, and you can remove it yourself:

  • Only study history: open the app → Settings → Clear Study History.
  • Everything: open the app → Settings → Delete All App Data, then type DELETE to confirm.
  • Remove the app: uninstall ParhaiMate. This deletes all of its data from the phone.
  • Android backup copy: if Android backup is on, a copy may be kept in your Google account. You can remove it in your phone's Settings → Google → Backup, or at Google Drive → Storage → Backups.
  • Exported files: backups and reports you exported are normal files. Delete them wherever you saved or shared them.

Common questions

My session alert was late. Android may delay alerts to save battery. Turning off battery optimisation for ParhaiMate in Android settings usually helps.

I got a new phone. Export a backup from Settings → Export Backup on the old phone and import it on the new one, or use Android's own backup and restore.
