**Full description** (max 4000):
```
ParhaiMate is a calm, offline study companion that helps you focus, plan your study routine and see your progress grow. No account, no ads, no internet needed.

FOCUS YOUR WAY
• Focus Timer – pick 15, 25, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or set your own
• Pomodoro – focus cycles with short breaks and a long break at the end
• Stopwatch – open-ended sessions when you just want to track time
• Session alerts tell you when a focus block, break or session ends
• Timers keep counting correctly even if you lock your phone or close the app

ORGANISE YOUR SUBJECTS
• Add subjects with simple icons and a weekly target
• See total time, session count and weekly progress for each subject
• Archive subjects you are no longer studying

PLAN YOUR STUDY
• Study planner with Today, Upcoming and Completed tasks
• Monthly calendar showing the days you studied and the tasks you planned
• Start a session straight from a planned task

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Daily and weekly study goals with clear progress rings
• Streaks to keep you consistent
• Weekly, monthly and yearly statistics with charts
• Subject breakdown, best study day and longest session
• Achievements for milestones like your first hour and a 7-day streak

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• Everything is stored on your phone
• Export a backup file and restore it any time, or move it to a new phone
• Export PDF and CSV study reports
• No sign-up, no tracking, no ads

ParhaiMate is built for school, college and university students who want a simple, distraction-free way to study better.
```
