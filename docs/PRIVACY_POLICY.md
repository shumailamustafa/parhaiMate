# ParhaiMate Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 20 November 2026
**App:** ParhaiMate — Your Personal Study Companion (Android)
**Developer:** Moazzam Samoo

ParhaiMate is an offline study app. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

## Summary

- No account or sign-in.
- No ads, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.
- The app does not send your data to any server run by us or anyone else.
- Your study data stays on your device, except for Android's own backup (explained below) and files you choose to export.

## Data stored on your device

ParhaiMate stores the following in a private database on your phone:

- Subjects you create (name, icon, weekly target)
- Study sessions (subject, mode, start and end time, duration, optional notes)
- Planner tasks, daily and weekly goals, streaks, achievements and timer presets
- App settings (for example default timer mode, and whether session alerts are on)

This data is used only to show your progress inside the app. It is not shared with anyone.

## Session alerts (notifications)

If you allow notifications, ParhaiMate schedules local alerts on your device for when a focus phase, break or study session ends. These alerts are created by the app on the phone itself; nothing is sent over the internet. You can turn alerts off in **Settings → Session Alerts** or in Android settings.

## Android Auto Backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may back up the ParhaiMate study database and settings to your Google account and restore them on a new device. This is handled by Android and Google under Google's privacy terms, not by ParhaiMate. ParhaiMate excludes caches, export copies and its own safety snapshots from this backup. You can turn backup off in your device's Android settings.

## Backups and reports you export

When you export a backup (JSON) or a study report (PDF or CSV), the file is created on your device and saved or shared only to the place you choose. Anyone who has the file can read it, so keep it somewhere safe. Before a backup replaces your current data, ParhaiMate keeps a private safety copy on your device (the last three are kept).

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — only to show the session alerts described above.
- **Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)** — only so pending session alerts are re-scheduled after the phone restarts.

ParhaiMate does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files outside the ones you pick.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Clear Study History** deletes all study sessions.
- **Settings → Delete All App Data** deletes everything the app stores.
- Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your device.

## Children

ParhaiMate is a general study tool and is not directed at children under 13.
